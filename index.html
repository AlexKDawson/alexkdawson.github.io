About

First Proof Labs is a software engineering practice founded by Alex Dawson. We partner with founders and teams to design, build, and ship reliable software — from early prototypes through production systems.

Based in the United States. Available for remote engagements worldwide.

Approach

  • Small teams. Senior engineers from day one. No layers, no handoffs.
  • Milestone-based. Clear scope, fixed checkpoints, working software at each step.
  • Production-minded. We build for the system that will exist in two years, not just the demo next month.
  • Honest assessments. If a project should be smaller, simpler, or shelved, we say so.

Work

Selected engagements span backend systems, developer tooling, AI/ML integration, and applied research. Detailed case studies available on request.
